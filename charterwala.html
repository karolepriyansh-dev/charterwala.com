Find. Compare. Book.

Charterwala shows real bus routes and fares collected from India’s top booking platforms.

View Buses

Data snapshot collected Nov 2026 from redBus, EaseMyTrip, Via.com, AbhiBus, Ixigo and Rome2rio and MakeMyTrip. Fares are “starting from” prices and some timings come from listing pages for an earlier travel date, so they change with date, demand and seat type.

Book a Bus

  1. Search a route or browse View Buses.
  2. Press Book Now on a bus. Charterwala opens the exact platform the data came from.
  3. Pick seats, boarding point and pay there. You get an e-ticket from that platform.

Cancel Booking

Cancellations are processed by the platform that issued your ticket, as per the operator’s policy.

About the Project

Problem
Travellers compare buses across many apps. Charterwala brings routes and fares into one clean view.
How it works
Route data is collected from public booking pages. Booking and payment are handed to the original platform, so Charterwala never touches payments.
Tech stack
HTML5, CSS3 (responsive grid, dark mode), vanilla JavaScript. Single file, no frameworks.
Future scope
Live data through a backend API or scraper (Node.js + database), user login, seat maps, fare alerts and price history.

Why a snapshot? A static page cannot safely fetch other sites’ data in the browser (CORS limits). A real-time version needs a server-side layer, which is the natural next step for this project.
